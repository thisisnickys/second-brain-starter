# Example — a decision worth remembering

Decision notes are the highest-value thing in a second brain. Six months from
now you will not remember *why* you chose something, and the argument will
restart from zero.

**Decision:** Keep every note in plain markdown instead of a database app.

**Why:** Files outlive apps. Any tool that can read text can read this brain,
and nothing can lock the data up or shut it down.

**What I gave up:** No pretty mobile app out of the box. Fixed later with the
Telegram bot.

## Apply

Next time you change your mind about something that costs money or time, write
it here in this shape: Decision / Why / What I gave up.
